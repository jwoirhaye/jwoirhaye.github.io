PhD Candidate on Cloud Computing

University of Lille | Inria - SPIRALS & STACK

I am a PhD candidate in Cloud Computing at Inria (Université de Lille), advised by Romain Rouvoy, Thomas Ledoux (IMT Atlantique), and Francescomaria Faticanti, as part of the PEPR CARECloud project. My research focuses on energy-efficient scheduling of serverless functions (FaaS) in resource-constrained cloud infrastructures. I study how execution environments (containers, microVMs, unikernels) and language runtimes affect energy consumption, with the goal of enabling orchestration that adapts to each function's workload profile. Passionate about cloud-native technologies, containers, and energy-aware distributed systems.
